How does license activation work? +
After purchasing on Gumroad, you receive a license key. Enter it the first time you open DropSync Pro to activate your access period. The app validates your key and unlocks all features for your chosen plan duration.
Does it require an internet connection? +
No! DropSync Pro is built offline-first. The dashboard, inventory, pricing calculator, and all core features work completely without internet. Only optional integrations (Firebase sync, EmailJS alerts) need a connection.
How is my data stored and kept private? +
By default, all data is stored in your browser's localStorage — only on your device. For cloud backup, connect your own Firebase account. PrimeBytes Soluzione has zero access to your Firebase data. We never store your business data on our servers.
What's the difference between the plans? +
All plans include the complete feature set. The difference is activation duration — 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year, or Lifetime. The Lifetime plan also includes all future feature updates for free, forever.
Can I upgrade from a shorter plan to Lifetime? +

What platforms does DropSync Pro support? +
DropSync Pro supports Shopify, Amazon, eBay, and TikTok Shop for platform fee calculations and pricing. The inventory and pricing engine works for any product regardless of where you sell.

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. Purchase the plan that suits you and if you're not satisfied within 7 days, simply request a full refund — no questions asked. The 1-Month plan at $9 is also a very low-risk entry point.
